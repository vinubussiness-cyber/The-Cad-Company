Our Machine-Building Process

From Process Study
to Production.

A controlled machine-building workflow covering concept, engineering, build, validation and factory handover.

01

Process Study

Understand the manufacturing process, production volume, cycle time, component characteristics and automation requirements.

02

Machine Concept

Develop the machine concept, operating sequence, automation architecture and overall configuration.

03

Detailed Engineering

Develop the mechanical, electrical, pneumatic and control systems required for the machine.

04

Manufacturing & Procurement

Manufacture machine structures and components and procure required automation and machine components.

05

Assembly & Integration

Integrate the mechanical, electrical, pneumatic and control systems into the complete machine.

06

Testing & Validation

Test functionality, cycle time, repeatability, safety and process performance before deployment.

07

Installation & Commissioning

Install and commission the machine at the customer's facility and verify production operation.

08

Production Handover

Complete documentation, operator training and production handover.
